Module 01 · Capital Mechanics

Navigating Trailing vs Static Drawdown

Most prop firm traders fail not because of strategy, but because they don't realize how drawdown floors move.

1.1 The Trailing Drawdown Trap

A trailing drawdown is not a fixed number. It ratchets up as your account's unrealized equity hits new highs — and it never comes back down.

With a 5% trailing drawdown on a $100k account, every new equity high lifts the floor to 5% below that peak. If your open equity peaks at $107,000, the floor locks at $102,000 — immediately, while the trade is still open.

Plain English: know where your drawdown floor sits before the trade — not after the equity peaks.

1.2 Trailing vs Static Balance Drawdown

The two drawdown models behave differently under open trades. Know which one your evaluation uses before you size a position.

Trailing Drawdown (intraday peak)

  • Floor rises with unrealized equity highs.
  • Re-trails up in real time as open P&L peaks.
  • Locks in at the peak once the trade closes.

Static Balance Drawdown

  • Floor stays locked to the initial or closed daily balance.
  • Fixed safety buffer that does not move with open P&L.
  • Easier to plan position sizing against.
On trailing-drawdown accounts, bank profits and be cautious holding into new equity highs — the floor follows you up.
Module 02 · Position Sizing

Risk Calibration & Drawdown Protection

Fixed risk per trade — never fixed lots. Size to the stop, not to the target.

2.1 Risk Per Phase

Your risk budget changes as you move from evaluation to funded. The discipline does not.

Evaluation Phase Risk

  • Risk per trade: 0.5% max
  • Max daily trades: 2
  • Max daily risk: 1.0%
  • Hard stop on 2 consecutive losses.

Funded Account Risk

  • Risk per trade: 0.25% – 0.35%
  • Scale to 0.5% only after +3% buffer banked.
  • No execution ±5 min around high-impact news.

Module 04 · Payouts & Scaling

Payout Protocols & Scale-Up Engineering

A funded account is a capital base to grow — not a slot machine to drain.

4.1 Payout Buffer & Withdrawal Discipline

Never request a payout the moment you are eligible. Build a 3% – 5% cushion above the payout threshold first, so a withdrawal doesn't drop you straight into a drawdown violation.

  1. Bank 3% – 5% above threshold before the first withdrawal request.
  2. Withdraw on a fixed schedule (e.g. monthly), never on impulse after a hot streak.
  3. Leave enough in the account to keep position sizing intact after the withdrawal.
Withdraw from profit, never from the safety buffer. If a payout would push you near the drawdown floor, wait.

4.2 Scale-Up Engineering

Scaling is about keeping risk constant while capital grows — not about getting bigger, faster.

When to scale up

  • +3% buffer banked.
  • Consistent win-rate over a full month.
  • No rule violations in the current period.

How to scale

  • 0.25% → 0.35% → 0.5% per trade, one step at a time.
  • Hold each step for a full week before increasing again.
  • Recompute lot size on the new capital every time.
Scale risk with evidence, not emotion. A string of wins is not a reason to size up the next morning.
Module 05 · USME VWAP Elite Suite

Session Execution Framework

Precision session anchors and institutional liquidity execution for index and commodity futures.

5.1 Centralized Order Flow & Session Anchors

Unlike decentralized FX or retail spot markets, futures volume flows through a centralized order book. Institutional execution algorithms (TWAP / VWAP) are benchmarked against VWAP to complete large orders with minimal market impact.

ETH Anchor · 18:00 EST

  • Overnight session sets the global baseline volume-weighted price.
  • Overnight highs / lows outside the SD bands = mean-reversion setups.

RTH Anchor · 09:30 EST

  • Cash-session open injects a surge of institutional liquidity.
  • Fresh dynamic support / resistance for daytime volatility.
VWAP is the benchmark the execution algos are judged against. Trade the bands, not the noise.

5.2 The VWAP Band Structure

Standard-deviation bands around the session VWAP act as dynamic fair-value bounds — the centerline is fair value, and each band outward marks where price is statistically stretched.

5.3 Instrument Execution Mechanics

Each futures market has its own microstructure. Match the band usage to the instrument, not the other way around.

Instrument Microstructure / Volatility Primary VWAP Band Usage
ES High liquidity, tight spread, mean-reverting character. Dynamic support / resistance at ±1.0σ & ±2.0σ during RTH consolidation.
NQ Higher beta, wide range expansion, momentum trend. Break above / below ±1.0σ signals session expansion toward ±2.5σ / 3.0σ pools.
CL Heavy inventory / news volatility, sharp order-flow sweeps. Session resets around the NYMEX pit open (09:00 EST) → mean-reversion off ±2.0σ spikes.

5.4 Prop Firm Strategy Implementation

Four locked steps, mapped to the prop-firm rule set — preserving max trailing drawdown, daily loss limits, and target consistency.

  1. Session anchor setup & reset. Dual VWAP anchors — ETH (18:00 EST) for overnight context, RTH (09:30 EST) for cash-session execution. Mark ±1.0σ / ±2.0σ / ±3.0σ on 1m / 3m / 5m.
  2. Filter the regime at the RTH open. Mean-reversion: price inside ±1.0σ with low displacement → fade at ±2.0σ, target dynamic VWAP. Trend-expansion: price pushes out of ±1.0σ on volume → retest VWAP / ±1.0σ as continuation entries.
  3. Execution & SMC confluence. Combine a band test with a local Fair Value Gap or Order Block. Enter on the 1-minute market-structure shift (MSS) after sweeping the ±2.0σ boundary.
  4. Prop-firm risk protocol. Stop beyond the structural high / low — max 8–12 ticks (ES), 15–25 ticks (NQ), 10–15 ticks (CL). TP1 at the VWAP centerline (lock 50% + breakeven), TP2 at the opposite ±1.5σ / ±2.0σ band. Daily guardrail: hard cutoff after 2 consecutive losses or at 50% of the daily loss limit.

Stop-Loss Matrix

Playbooks · Two execution strategies

Prop Evaluation Playbooks

Numbered rules in plain English. Same language you use at the desk.

A Conservative 0.5% Consistency Protocol

Designed to systematically hit the 8 – 10% profit target without triggering daily or trailing drawdown limits.

  1. Trend Cloud BULL or BEAR only — identify HTF bias via the Triple-EMA Trend Cloud. No range.
  2. Wait for a London or NY AM kill-zone sweep of BSL / SSL.
  3. Confirm the 3-candle non-repainting FVG retest in discount (for long) or premium (for short).
  4. Size the position to exactly 0.5% account risk from the stop distance.
  5. TP1 at 1:1.5 R:R — lock 50% of the position, trail the rest to the opposing liquidity pool.
Consistency beats aggression. This playbook wins by surviving, not by hero trades.

B High-Confluence Breaker Surge

Higher win-rate setup for clearing Phase 2 or recovering after a minor drawdown.

  1. Sweep of the major session high/low during NY PM macro (13:30 – 16:00 EST).
  2. Rapid displacement breaking internal market structure (MSS).
  3. Entry on the Breaker Block retest with Anchored VWAP alignment.
  4. Fixed 0.25% risk per trade with a 1:2 R:R target.
Recovery mode is not revenge mode. Keep risk at 0.25% until the buffer is rebuilt.

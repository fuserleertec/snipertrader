Module 01 · Capital Mechanics

Navigating Trailing vs Static Drawdown

Understanding the Trailing Drawdown Trap

Most prop firm traders fail not due to strategy, but because they do not realize trailing drawdowns move up with intra-trade unrealized equity high points.

Trailing Drawdown (Intraday Peak) As open unrealized equity hits +$2,000, your maximum allowed drawdown floor moves up by +$2,000 immediately.
Static Balance Drawdown Drawdown floor stays locked based on initial balance or daily closed balance, offering a fixed safety buffer.
Module 02 · Position Sizing

Risk Calibration & Drawdown Protection

Evaluation Phase Risk

  • • Risk per trade: 0.5% max
  • • Max daily trades: 2 trades
  • • Max daily risk allocation: 1.0%
  • • Hard stop on 2 consecutive losses.

Funded Account Risk

  • • Risk per trade: 0.25% - 0.35%
  • • Scale risk to 0.5% only after +3% buffer is banked.
  • • Zero trade execution during High Impact News (±5 min).

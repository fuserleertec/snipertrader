The 3-Gate Core

From regime to managed path. In three gates.

Meridian OFI never takes a trade off one signal. Three gates must clear in order — a regime-aware bias, a liquidity arm, and an untapped zone retest — before the engine commits a managed path from entry to TP3.

Gate 01

Bias — regime-aware

The engine classifies every bar TREND, RANGE, or TRANSITION. In TREND it trades only with the ribbon and cloud; in RANGE it fades premium and discount extremes; in TRANSITION it demands a denser lattice before anything fires.

Gate 02

Armed — liquidity swept

A setup only arms on a confirmed liquidity sweep, a volume-backed VWAP reclaim, or a bullish/bearish CHoCH. The arm expires after a preset number of bars — stale setups are dropped automatically.

Gate 03

Zone retest — lattice graded

Price must tap an untapped FVG (or order block) while the event lattice reads at least your minimum grade. No-chase blocks stretched entries in TREND; RANGE gets a fade-into-extremes exception.

Manage

Path — entry to TP3

On entry, Meridian OFI plots a structural stop and three targets. PATH CONFIRM fires when TP1 is touched; PATH BREAK fires if price closes beyond the stop first. One path per direction, tracked live.

Mutual exclusion built in. If both long and short fire on the same bar, Meridian OFI keeps the higher-density side — or stands aside entirely on a tie. It will not hand you a conflicting pair.

Order Flow Intelligence

Volume's true intent. On the lattice.

The OFI layer reads cumulative volume delta and projects it onto the same SMC/VWAP/magnet levels the engine already tracks. Absorption (type 8) and imbalance (type 9) join the lattice as direction-aware events — and v2.2 adds stacked imbalance (type 10), read from TradingView's native diagonal footprint. All three feed straight into density → grade → Gate 3 with no new gates.

⇅
Primitive

Cumulative Volume Delta

close-vs-prior-close split · reset None / Session / Daily

Every bar's volume is split up/down by close vs. prior close — robust on feeds without true bid-ask — then accumulated into a running CVD. A 3-bar slope sets the rising/falling state; session high and low are tracked live.

Absorption

strong opposing delta + rejection at a level

Large opposing volume that fails to continue — price stalls or rejects at an FVG/order block, a magnet, or a VWAP band. A bullish close on heavy selling delta marks demand absorbing supply; a bearish close on heavy buying delta marks supply absorbing demand.

Type 10

Stacked Imbalance

native diagonal footprint · ≥3 consecutive rows · proxy fallback

request.footprint() reads true bid/ask at every price row. A buy imbalance flags when a row's buy volume ≥ threshold% of the sell volume of the row below it (diagonal, 3:1 default); a sell imbalance mirrors it above. Three or more consecutive same-side rows form a stacked zone — the strongest initiative read in the lattice, pushed as type 10 and optionally registered as a high-intensity magnet.

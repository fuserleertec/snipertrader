The 3-Gate Model

From context to entry. In three gates.

A signal is only valid when every gate is true on a closed bar. If any gate fails, the setup is ignored. This is the entire logic — no percentage score, no hidden weighting.

Gate 01 · Context

Trade with the higher-timeframe bias — or not at all.

The higher-timeframe EMA (default 1H / 50) sets the directional bias from a confirmed HTF close — never a forming bar. Buys require a bullish bias, sells a bearish one. The London (02:00–05:00) and NY AM (09:30–12:00) session windows keep you out of dead hours, and premium/discount (a rolling 60-bar range) forces longs in discount and shorts in premium.

HTF bias (non-repaint)Session filterPremium / Discount
Gate 02 · Liquidity

Wait for smart money to leave a footprint.

A liquidity sweep (wick through a prior extreme, body closing back inside), a displacement candle (full range > ATR × 1.5), and a Market Structure Shift (close through the prior swing on displacement) prove the move is institutional, not noise.

Sweep (BSL / SSL)Displacement ×ATRMSS
Gate 03 · Entry

Enter on a retrace into a fresh imbalance.

Price retraces 50% into the Fair Value Gap (or Order Block fallback if no FVG exists), with session VWAP as confluence — longs only below VWAP, shorts only above. The signal fires once per setup and carries its own ATR stop, TP1 (1R) and TP2 (2R).

The Engine

Every component. One purpose.

Eight clean pieces stack into the 3-gate filter. Each one maps to a specific row in the dashboard — so what you see on the chart is exactly what the code is checking.

Gate 01

HTF bias — non-repainting

A higher-timeframe EMA with lookahead off. It reads only the confirmed HTF close and the EMA's direction, so the bias line never wiggles mid-bar. Bull = close above a rising EMA; bear = close below a falling EMA.

Gate 01

Session + premium/discount

Only the London (02:00–05:00) and NY AM (09:30–12:00) kill zones trade. A rolling 60-bar range draws the equilibrium — longs require discount, shorts require premium. No more forcing trades in the wrong part of the range or in off-hours chop.

Gate 02

Sweep + displacement

A liquidity sweep needs a wick through the prior 20-bar extreme with the body closing back inside (BSL / SSL). A displacement candle needs its full range to exceed ATR × 1.5 — the institutional footprint that separates a real move from chop.

BSL / SSL×ATR 1.5
⇪
Gate 02

Market Structure Shift

An MSS fires when a displacement candle closes through the prior swing high or low (5-bar swing). It's the confirmation that structure — not just a wick — has changed, and it's what upgrades a sweep into a directional signal.

5-bar swingdisplacement-confirmed
▦
Gate 03

FVG / OB zones

A Fair Value Gap forms when a displacement candle leaves a 3-candle imbalance (low[1] > high[3]). It persists until filled — not reset at midnight. If no FVG exists, an Order Block (the last opposing candle within 10 bars) is used as the fallback entry zone.

FVG preferredOB fallbackpersist till filled

Gate 03

Session VWAP + risk

Session VWAP (reset daily) acts as the final confluence filter — longs only below it, shorts only above. Every entry carries a systematic ATR(14) × 1.5 stop beyond the zone, a TP1 at 1R and TP2 at 2R, plus a risk readout in points.

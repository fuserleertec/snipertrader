> `⚠️ MODE DIVERGENCE` callouts:
> - **EXTERNAL** — sell full-service video marketing to other trading brands/prop firms/educators (retainer-based, billable clients).
> - **INTERNAL** — use the same playbook to scale snipertrader.ai's own subscribers, course sales, and tool signups (internal budget, not billable).
> Both share the same playbook, SOPs, stack, and "Noise→Precision" narrative. The
> only real differences: money direction (invoice out vs budget in), risk (client

You are not a "video agency." You sell *precision*. The narrative arc every asset follows:

```
NOISE (status quo)            PRECISION (your outcome)
─────────────────            ─────────────────────────
Random scrollers             Verified setups
Hype "lambo" clips           Backtested, timestamped entries
Founder does everything      Repeatable content + ad engine
CAC rising, trust falling    CAC down, LTV up, community compounding
```

**Proof pole you stand on:** `snipertrader.ai` itself. The Kronos Foundation Model's
real OHLCV forecasts, the USME_ICT_Calculator prints, and Traderedge's AI guardrails
are the living case study — *"We don't pitch theory; our own algorithm prints signals live."*

### 1.3 Core Offer — "Precision-Growth Architecture"
A 3-layer system that turns a trading founder's complexity into compounding content + paid acquisition:

1. **Signal-to-Story Engine** — convert live entries, backtests, indicator prints into short/long-form video.
2. **Trust Funnel** — landing page + Telegram/Discord capture + email/SMS trial-to-paid.
3. **Paid Amplification** — Meta/YouTube/X ads scaling only the winners (whitelisted creatives).

> ⚠️ **MODE DIVERGENCE**

## 2. Core Service Stack & Service Delivery

### 2.1 Video Content Engine
| Format | Spec | Trading-specific angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Short-form execution breakdowns** | 15–45s, 3/wk/client | Live entry + SL/TP, "here's the ICT Judas swing print" |
| **Long-form YouTube** | 12–25 min, 1–2/mo | Backtesting marathon, platform walkthrough of Kronos/Traderedge |
| **High-production VSL** | 6–12 min | The founder story + proof + offer stack |

### 2.2 Full-Service Ecosystem
- **Paid Ad Management** — Meta (Reels ads), YouTube (in-stream + Shorts), X (promoted).
- **Community Growth Funnels** — Telegram/Discord onboarding, gated "signal room" as lead magnet.
- **Landing Page Copy & UI** — Framer/Webflow, built on the canonical `assets/theme.css` green-cyan system for brand consistency.
- **Email/SMS Automation** — trial → paid drip (7-touch), win-back.

> ⚠️ **MODE DIVERGENCE**
> - **EXTERNAL:** Each bullet is a *line item* you deliver to a client; ad spend is pass-through + management fee.
> - **INTERNAL:** These are *your departments*. Ad spend comes from snipertrader.ai's growth budget; "client success" = your own subscriber retention.

### 2.3 SOP Framework — "Capture → Cut → Ship" (5-day turnaround)

**SOP-01: Screen-Recorded Setup Capture**
1. Founder runs TradingView + `USME_ICT_Calculator` / Kronos dashboard, records 3–5 min raw.
2. Drop into Descript; auto-transcribe; mark the "entry moment" + "why" (ICT concept named).
3. Editor clips to 30s, adds: timestamp overlay, SL/TP lines, concept label (e.g., "FVG reclaim").
4. Export 1:1 (Reels), 9:16 (TikTok/Shorts), caption with ticker + concept hashtag.

**SOP-02: Remote Founder Podcast**
1. Riverside/Zoom record (1080p), separate tracks.
2. AI remove filler words (Descript), cut to 18-min YouTube + 4 clips.
3. Thumbnail: chart still + bold claim ("Why 90% of funded challenges fail").

**SOP-03: Visual Backtest Overlay**
1. Pull CSV of trades (or Kronos OHLCV export) → PineScript replay.
2. Animate entries/exits as on-chart markers; add equity-curve lower pane.
3. Sync voiceover explaining edge/win-rate/expectancy.

## 3. Pricing & Revenue Model

### 3.1 Tiered Retainers
| Tier | Price/mo | Deliverables | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Precision Builder** | $5,000 | 8 shorts + 1 long + 1 landing page/mo, no paid mgmt | Early educators, indie tool founders |
| **Scale Engine** | $10,000 | 12 shorts + 2 long + VSL + Meta/YT ad mgmt (up to $10k spend) | Prop firms, established educators |
| **Institutional Growth** | $18,000+ | Full stack + rev-share (5–10%) or ad-spend % (10–15% mgmt fee) | Protocols, fintech SaaS, funded programs |

> ⚠️ **MODE DIVERGENCE**
> - **EXTERNAL:** These are *client invoices*. Rev-share tier aligns you with client revenue (only offer once you've proven lift).
> - **INTERNAL:** These become *budget tiers you allocate to yourself* — i.e., "Phase 1: invest $5k/mo equivalent in content ops; Phase 3: $18k/mo equivalent incl. ad spend." No invoice, just P&L line items.

### 3.2 Unit Economics (EXTERNAL model, per client)
- Blended delivery cost at Scale Engine ≈ $4,200 (editor $2.5k + media buyer $1.2k + copy $500).
- **Gross margin ≈ 58%** at $10k tier.
- Target **LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1**. With $10k/mo and 6-mo avg retention → $60k LTV; CAC (outbound + content) target < $20k.
- Rev-share clients: model 12-mo LTV, cap exposure on ad spend.

### 3.3 LTV Optimization Levers
1. **Community lock-in** — Discord/Telegram paid tier raises stickiness (churn ↓).
2. **Content moat** — 6+ mo of backtest library makes switching costly.
3. **Upsell path** — Builder → Engine → Institutional as client MRR grows.

> ⚠️ **MODE DIVERGENCE**

> - **INTERNAL:** You already own most of this (the site, the tools, Kronos backend). Stack cost ≈ near-zero incremental → your "agency margin" is effectively your *own* saved CAC.

## 5. Client Acquisition & Outreach Strategy

### 5.1 Outreach Blueprint — "Trading Funnel Audit" (video teardown)
- Target: trading-tool founders, Discord/Telegram owners (1k–50k members), prop-firm founders.
- Hook: record a 60–90s teardown of *their* funnel or last 3 posts → show one "precision" fix.
- Send personalized, not blast.

**Cold Email Template (Trading Funnel Audit):**
```
Subject: Tore down your last 3 Reels — 1 fix = cleaner CAC

Hey [Name],

Saw [TheirBrand]'s "Funded Challenge" Reel series — clean entries, but
the CTA sends cold traffic straight to a $997 course. That's leaking
trial-stage buyers.

I recorded a 75-second teardown showing how a 7-day free "signal room"
hook (Telegram) would capture the 80% who aren't ready to pay yet:
[loom link]

I run SniperTrader Media — we turn trading setups into Precision-Growth
funnels for prop firms and educators. If the teardown's useful, happy
to show what the full architecture looks like for [TheirBrand].

[Your name]
SniperTrader Media — From Noise to Precision Execution
```

### 5.2 Inbound Engine (your unfair advantage)
- `snipertrader.ai`'s **Kronos live forecasts** + **Traderedge AI guardrails** = proof no other agency can show.
- Publish a weekly "Signal Breakdown" short using real Kronos prints → ranks for "[ticker] forecast" searches.
- Case-study page: the `snipertrader_media.html` landing page (built on canonical theme) shows the architecture in action.

### 5.3 Closing Script Framework (high-ticket, ROI/CAC-led)
1. **Diagnose (numbers first):** "What's your current CAC and trial→paid rate?"
2. **Mirror the leak:** Tie their number to a specific content/funnel gap.
3. **Architecture pitch:** Show the 3-layer system; name the expected CAC drop.
4. **Risk reversal:** "Start at Precision Builder; we graduate you to Scale Engine only after we prove a 20% CAC lift."
5. **Close on math:** "At $10k/mo, if we drop CAC 20%, you net [X] — ROI-positive in week [N]."

> ⚠️ **MODE DIVERGENCE**
> - **EXTERNAL:** This is your *sales motion to paying clients*; the "case study" is fabricated-for-client until you have real ones (use your own as the prototype).
> - **INTERNAL:** You are both sides of the table — run the teardown on *competitors'* funnels publicly (inbound content), and use the closing math on your *own* budget approvals.

## 6. Team Structure & Hiring Roadmap

### 6.1 Lean Core (0 → $30k MRR)

- **Community/Growth Manager** — runs Telegram/Discord, owns activation.

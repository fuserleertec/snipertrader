What is Kronos?

Think of Kronos as an AI model designed specifically for financial charts. Just as language models learn to predict the next word in a sentence, Kronos reads past chart patterns to forecast where price is likely to head next.

By analyzing massive amounts of historical chart data across 45 global exchanges, Kronos spots deep market patterns that traditional technical indicators miss.

>How to Use the Interactive Workspace

  • Load Your Data: Feed the chart up to 512 candles of historical price action (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume).
  • Generate Projections: Kronos simulates multiple potential forward price paths based on historical probabilities.
  • Analyze the Forecast: View the most likely median price path along with shaded confidence bands to gauge risk, potential volatility, and price targets.

    • Why It Matters

  • Built-in Risk Assessment: Visual confidence bands show you high-probability zones, helping you manage risk before entering a trade.
  • Institutional-Grade AI: Leverages pre-trained market intelligence without requiring complex setup or coding.

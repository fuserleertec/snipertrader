Non-Repaint Pivots

Learn locked swing levels that stay put after the bar closes — so you trade what you see, not hindsight.

This course is for beginners. We use short sentences. Each new word is explained once, then reused.

Noise → lock

A forming candle is noise. A finished candle can lock a level.

Patience first

Wait for the close. If you rush, you trade a picture that can still change.

Edge is process

Same rules, every time: lock, confirm, size the risk, then act.

Words we will use

A pivot is a swing high or swing low — a turning point on the chart.

A candle (also called a bar) is one block of price for a set time. When that time ends, the candle is closed.

A repaint is a marker that moves or vanishes after you first saw it. A locked marker stays put.

Module 01
Indicator Fundamentals & Setup

See it clear. Then lock it in.

First you learn why most pivot tools lie after the fact. Then you set the indicator so it only speaks on a finished candle.

Lesson 1.1

Repaint vs Locked

Many pivot tools shift as new ticks arrive. The dot slides. The line redraws. The old signal disappears. That is a repaint. It is a hindsight trap. The chart looks perfect later. It did not look that way in real time.

A non-repaint pivot waits until the candle finishes. Only then can the marker appear. Once it appears, it does not move. It does not vanish.

Think of it this way: do not trust a candle that is still being drawn. Wait until it is done. Then the level can lock.

Closed bar only

If the candle is still open, the story can still change. Locked pivots confirm after the close — the same idea as “wait until the candle finishes.”

Lesson 1.2

Setup & Parameters

You only need two settings to start. Leave the rest alone until you can read a lock.

Pivot depth is how many candles sit on each side of a swing. A deeper number needs a bigger turn before it locks. Use about 10 for scalping on the 5-minute or 15-minute chart. Use about 21 for swing trades on the 1-hour or 4-hour chart.

ATR means Average True Range. It is a simple measure of how much price usually moves. An ATR multiplier stretches or tightens bands around the pivot. In a quiet market the bands sit closer. In a wild market they sit farther. The bands adapt to volatility.

Always calculate on closed bars only. If the candle is still forming, wait.

Depth ~10

Scalp setting. Faster locks on 5m and 15m charts.

Depth ~21

Swing setting. Slower, larger turns on 1H and 4H.

ATR bands

Distance from the pivot follows recent volatility, not a fixed number of points.

Module 02
Reading Signals

The close is the vote.

A lock is not a trade by itself. You still need the candle to finish on the right side of the level.

Lesson 2.1

Reversal vs Breakout

Support is a price where buyers have held before. A support pivot is a locked low.

Resistance is a price where sellers have held before. A resistance pivot is a locked high.

Bullish · long bias

Price touches locked support. Then the candle closes above that support. That is a long bias — you are only looking to buy.

Bearish · short bias

Price touches locked resistance. Then the candle closes below that resistance. That is a short bias — you are only looking to sell.

After the bar closes, check the label. It should stay locked. If it stays, the signal is real. If it slides or vanishes, it was never locked.

Lesson 2.2

Filter Fakeouts

A fakeout is a move that looks like a signal, then fails. Mid-bar, price can poke through a pivot and then snap back. That poke is not a vote.

Use the closed-candle rule only. Do not enter while the candle is still open.

Wait for the lock. If the signal disappears before the close, skip it. That was a repaint-style trap.

Patience is the filter. No lock, no trade.

Skip rule

If the marker is not locked at the close, there is no setup. Do not chase the wick. Wait for the next finished candle.

Module 03
Execution & Risk

Define risk first.

A locked signal still needs a plan. You decide the entry, the stop, and the target before you click.

Lesson 3.1

Stops & Targets

The signal bar is the candle that closed on the right side of the locked pivot. You do not enter on that close. You enter on the next bar open.

A stop-loss is the price where the idea is wrong. For a long, place it 1.5 × ATR below the pivot low. For a short, place it 1.5 × ATR above the pivot high. The extra room is a buffer so normal noise does not stop you out.

A take-profit is where you bank the trade. Use the next major pivot, or 2.5 × ATR from entry.

Reward is 2.5 × ATR. Risk is 1.5 × ATR. That is about 1.67 to 1. You do not need a perfect ratio. You do need a written plan.

01

Entry

Next bar open after the confirmed signal bar.

02

Stop

1.5 × ATR below the pivot low (longs) or above the pivot high (shorts).

03

Target

Next major pivot, or 2.5 × ATR from entry.

Lesson 3.2

Multi-Timeframe

A timeframe is the candle size you watch. A 5-minute chart makes a new candle every five minutes. A 1-hour chart makes one candle each hour.

The higher timeframe sets direction. Here that is the 1-hour chart. The lower timeframe times the entry. Here that is the 5-minute chart.

Align 5-minute pivot entries with the 1-hour trend. Take longs only when the higher timeframe agrees. If the 1-hour is up, look for locked support bounces on the 5-minute. If they disagree, wait.

Module 04
Complementary Filters

Extra checks. Not a new system.

A filter is an extra yes or no. It does not replace the locked pivot. It only supports it. Skip a filter if you are still learning the lock.

200 EMA

An EMA is an exponential moving average — a smoothed line of recent prices. The 200 EMA is a slow trend line. Longs only above it. Shorts only below it.

RSI / Stochastic

RSI is the Relative Strength Index, a 0–100 stretch meter. Stochastic is similar. Divergence means price makes a new low (or high) while the oscillator does not. At a locked pivot, that is confluence — two reasons to care.

ATR

Use ATR to trail a stop as the trade works. Also check whether ATR is rising on a breakout. Rising ATR means the move has energy.

Key Takeaways

Lock it in. Then trade the plan.

  • Repaint markers move. Locked markers stay after the candle closes.
  • Depth 10 for scalps. Depth 21 for swings. Always wait for the close.
  • Longs: close above locked support. Shorts: close below locked resistance.
  • Enter on the next open. Stop 1.5 × ATR. Target 2.5 × ATR or the next pivot.
  • Trade with the higher timeframe. Filters are optional extra checks.

Practice on replay. Protect capital first.

Replay the same five steps until they feel slow and boring. That is the point.

01

Algorithmic Liquidity & Displacement

We score impulse legs with a body-to-wick ratio plus ATR expansion. Only real impulse legs mark structure — not every tiny candle.

Liquidity = resting stop orders above highs / below lows. Displacement = a strong impulsive move that leaves imbalance.

02

Non-Repainting FVGs

A Fair Value Gap (FVG) is a price gap / imbalance left by a strong move. We use a strict 3-candle rule:

Bull: Low3 > High1  |  Bear: High3 < Low1

States: Mitigated · Partially Filled (50% CE) · Invalidation.

03

Order Block & Breaker Matrix

An Order Block (OB) is the last opposing candle before displacement. When that OB breaks, it becomes a Breaker — the old support/resistance flips role.

Module 01 · Liquidity & Structure

Liquidity & Structural Pivots

Locked NoRepaint pivots mark where stops rest. Sweeps of those pools often precede reversals.

1.1 Non-Repaint Pivot Arrows

A pivot is a swing high or swing low. Traditional markers often repaint — they move or vanish as new ticks arrive. That is a hindsight trap.

SniperTrader NoRepaint pivots use Lookback 5 / Confirm 2: five bars of structure on the left, two closed bars on the right. Once the arrow prints after the bar closes, it stays locked.

  • Lookback 5 — enough left-side bars so noise does not count as a swing.
  • Confirm 2 — two closed right-side bars before the pivot is accepted.
  • Bar-close only — no mid-candle markers.
Plain English: wait for the candle to finish. If the arrow is still there after the close, it is locked.

1.2 Buy-Side & Sell-Side Liquidity

Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) = resting stop orders above prior highs (shorts get stopped → market buys into those stops).

Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) = resting stop orders below prior lows (longs get stopped → market sells into those stops).

Practical rule: identify a liquidity sweep above a prior high or below a prior low before expecting a reversal. The sweep grabs the stops; the reverse is the trade idea.

Sweep then reverse. Do not fade the first touch of liquidity — wait for the grab and the close back inside.
Module 02 · Displacement & Gaps

Displacement & FVG Mapping

Trade fair value gaps only with the Triple-EMA Trend Cloud — never against it.

2.1 Triple-EMA Trend Cloud

The Trend Cloud is your directional filter. Green = bull. Red = bear. Grey = stand aside.

  • BULL (green) — EMA9 > EMA21 and Close > EMA200 → longs only.
  • BEAR (red) — EMA9 < EMA21 and Close < EMA200 → shorts only.
  • RANGE (grey) — neither → no directional edge.
Filter: long FVGs only when Trend Cloud is BULL. Short FVGs only when BEAR. Never fade the cloud.

2.2 Dynamic FVG Shading

After displacement, a 3-candle imbalance prints. Dynamic shading tracks the gap until it is mitigated, half-filled at the 50% CE (consequent encroachment = midpoint of the FVG), or invalidated.

  • Unmitigated — gap still open; valid for retest entries.
  • Partially filled (50% CE) — price tagged the midpoint; still usable with care.
  • Mitigated / Invalidated — gap closed or broken; drop it from the playbook.
Module 03 · Fair Value

Institutional Fair Value

Anchored VWAP with sigma bands tells you premium vs discount — buy discount in bullish structure.

3.1 Anchored VWAP ±1σ / ±2σ / ±3σ

VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) is a fair-value line from your chosen anchor. Deviation bands (±1σ, ±2σ, ±3σ) show how stretched price is from that value.

  • Below VWAP / −1σ — discount zone in a bullish market.
  • Above VWAP / +1σ — premium zone in a bearish market.
  • ±3σ — statistical extreme; watch for exhaustion or strong continuation.
Practical: buy in discount (below VWAP / −1σ) during bullish structure. Sell in premium during bearish structure.
Module 04 · Execution

Execution & Signal Confluence

Bar-close alerts only. Structural stops and targets. Three-confluence alignment before risk.

4.1 Bar-Close Triggers & Stop/Target Matrix

Execute strictly on candle close after 3-confluence alignment. Mid-bar wicks lie. Closed bars tell the truth.

Stop matrix

  • Long SL → below locked pivot low
  • Short SL → above locked pivot high / breaker high
  • Buffer for noise; never widen mid-trade

Target matrix

  • TP1 → nearest opposing liquidity or +1σ VWAP
  • TP2 → deeper pool or +3σ VWAP
  • Trail only after TP1 if structure holds

4.2 3-Confluence Checklist

All three must agree on the closed bar before you risk capital:

1. Trend — Trend Cloud BULL or BEAR (not RANGE).
2. Structure — Liquidity sweep + locked NoRepaint pivot / breaker context.
3. Value — FVG retest and/or VWAP discount/premium alignment.
4. Time (bonus) — Prefer London / NY AM kill zones when possible.
5. Trigger — Alert or signal confirmed on bar close — then place order.
If one of the three core filters disagrees, you wait. Precision over urgency.
Playbooks · Two strategies

Strategy Playbooks

Numbered rules in plain English. Same language you use at the desk.

A Premium/Discount FVG Retest with Anchored VWAP

Long setup: buy a bullish FVG retest in discount while the cloud is green and sell-side liquidity has been swept.

  1. Trend Cloud BULL (green) — EMA9 > EMA21 and Close > EMA200.
  2. Liquidity sweep of Sell-Side Liquidity below a locked NoRepaint Pivot Low.
  3. Displacement closes back above VWAP and leaves an unmitigated Bullish FVG.
  4. Limit entry at the top of the Bullish FVG (or 50% CE) — still below VWAP (discount).
  5. Risk — SL below the locked pivot low. TP1 at +1σ VWAP. TP2 at +3σ VWAP.
Discount + bullish FVG + green cloud + SSL sweep = the long confluence stack.

B Liquidity Sweep Breaker Block Reversal

Short setup: sweep buy-side liquidity, break structure, flip the last up-candle into a Breaker, short the retest.

  1. Trend Cloud RANGE → BEAR (grey → red). Bias flips to shorts.
  2. Sweep Buy-Side Liquidity above a locked NoRepaint Pivot High.
  3. Drop breaks prior swing low — the last up-candle before the drop becomes a Breaker Block.
  4. Short on retest of the Breaker lower boundary, with a close below the 9/21 EMA stack.
  5. Risk — SL above Breaker high. TP at the nearest unmitigated Sell-Side Liquidity.
Breaker = broken order block. Old demand becomes supply. Short the retest, not the first break.

Five takeaways. Then practice.

  • Liquidity = stops above highs / below lows. Sweep first, reverse second.
  • NoRepaint pivots lock after Lookback 5 / Confirm 2 — trust closed bars only.
  • FVGs are 3-candle gaps. Trade them with the Trend Cloud, never against it.
  • Buy discount below VWAP in bull structure; sell premium in bear structure.
  • Three-confluence + bar-close trigger. If one filter disagrees, you wait.

Practice on replay. Protect capital first. Education builds calm, surgical conviction — not urgency.

ABOUT THIS TERMINAL STATIC SNAPSHOT

Welcome to the SniperTrader market desk — one terminal, two scanners. This is the Decision Surface: it watches the biggest stocks, the major cryptocurrencies, and the key futures contracts, pulls each one's recent price history, and reads that price action to judge which way each market is leaning and how strongly. It then ranks every market from highest to lowest conviction and, for each, maps out where a reasonable entry, a protective stop, and a profit target would sit. The ranked list scrolls in place — the strongest setups sit up top, scroll for the rest. Click any row to open the full read — the reasoning, the exact levels, and a live chart. The Pre-Run Radar tab runs a separate, wider scan for names quietly building toward a move.

Here's What This Radar Actually Does

The plain-English version — what the pipeline really does, and where its limits are.

UniverseSweeps ~350 stocks across every size — large-cap down to small-cap and OTC — plus 24 liquid Binance crypto pairs, all on free public data.
ScoringScores each name on a blend of trend structure, trading volume, insider filings, and news catalysts, with a bounded directional projection folded in.
ConfirmationRuns a second-pass volume-and-momentum gate before anything is surfaced — failures go to a rejected list, never dropped silently.
OutputA short, ranked watchlist with plain-English reasons, conviction, position size, and stop/target levels — all simulated.
AlertsFlags dump / exit setups — overheated or fading-volume moves — so a late chase is visible before you take it.
HonestyEvery input it can't reach (order flow, dark pool, whale flows) is listed under Excluded Inputs — reported, never estimated.
No ordersRadar, not a broker. Everything is research and simulation; no orders are ever placed and no edge is claimed.

Module 01 · Microstructure & SMT

Cross-Market SMT Divergence & Liquidity Profiling

Centralized futures operate as one interconnected liquidity network. Institutional algorithms create divergence between correlated benchmarks before violent trend accelerations.

1.1 Smart Money Technique (SMT) Mechanics

Smart Money Technique (SMT) reads the disagreement between two correlated markets. When NQ prints a Higher High (HH) while ES only makes a Lower High (LH) at a key USME VWAP ±2.0σ band, it signals institutional distribution in NQ and absorption in ES.

The instrument that fails to confirm the move — the one leaving the lower high — is being used to distribute into strength. That is the divergence, and it is the signal.

SMT reads divergence, not direction. The market that refuses to confirm is telling you where smart money is selling into the rally.

1.2 Session Liquidity Profiles

Liquidity is delivered on a schedule. Each session prints a distinct profile — know which one you are trading.

Asia / London Sweep · 02:00–05:00 EST

  • Liquidity accumulation outside overnight value parameters.
  • Quiet sweeps of the overnight high / low outside ±1σ.
  • Sets the range the NY open will either accept or reject.

NY Open Injection · 09:30 EST

  • High-volume algorithm re-calibration with fresh session anchors.
  • RTH VWAP re-anchors — overnight levels are re-priced.
  • First 45 minutes = the highest-probability overextension window.
The 09:30 EST injection re-anchors VWAP — stale overnight levels are re-priced in the first minutes of the cash session.
Module 02 · Execution Frameworks

High-Velocity Session Execution Frameworks

Two institutional execution frameworks — one for range / mean-reverting regimes, one for high-beta trend expansion.

2.1 Strategy 1 · RTH Open Standard Deviation Reversion

Designed for range-bound / mean-reverting regimes during the NY Morning Session (09:30–11:30 EST).

  1. Establish the session anchor baseline. Reset the USME VWAP Elite anchor strictly at 09:30 EST. Mark static ±1.0σ / ±2.0σ / ±3.0σ expansion bands on the 3-minute timeframe.
  2. Detect institutional overextension. Watch for an aggressive directional spike that pushes price completely outside the ±2.0σ or ±3.0σ band within the first 45 minutes of RTH.
  3. USME ICT Core structural shift. Wait for a 1-minute Market Structure Shift (MSS) back inside the ±2.0σ band, leaving a clear Fair Value Gap (FVG) or Order Block (OB).
  4. Order execution & risk calibration. Enter via limit order at the 50% Consequent Encroachment (CE) of the FVG. Stop 2 ticks beyond the extreme sweep high / low. TP1 at the dynamic VWAP mean (scale 60%); TP2 at the opposite ±1.5σ band.
Reversion wins in range regimes — wait for the MSS back inside the band before fading the extreme. Never fade a band that is still breaking.

2.2 Strategy 2 · Cross-Correlated SMT Trend Continuation

Designed for high-beta trend-expansion regimes in index futures (NQ / ES).

  1. Confirm trend-extension alignment. Price holds consistently above (bullish) or below (bearish) the dynamic RTH VWAP centerline, with the 9/21 EMA cloud expanding in alignment.
  2. Identify inter-market SMT divergence. During a shallow retracement toward the dynamic +1.0σ or −1.0σ VWAP band, check for SMT divergence between ES and NQ.
  3. Execute on ICT Core displacement. Identify a nested FVG on the 2-minute timeframe that directly overlaps the dynamic ±1.0σ boundary. Enter immediately on a touch of the FVG threshold.
  4. Trail via institutional liquidity expansion. Stop below the swing low created during SMT formation. Target unmitigated buy-side / sell-side liquidity pools beyond the ±3.0σ band extension.
Continuation is a trend-follow, not a fade. Only trade the SMT divergence when the macro VWAP direction and the EMA cloud agree.

3.1 Overnight ETH Reversion Engine

The third profile in the suite — a low-volatility engine for the Asian–London drift, before the NY open injects volatility.

  1. Anchor the ETH VWAP at 18:00 EST and mark the ±1.0σ overnight band.
  2. During the 02:00–05:00 EST drift, wait for a quiet sweep of the overnight high / low outside ±1.0σ.
  3. Fade the sweep back toward the ETH VWAP mean with a tight stop beyond the sweep.
  4. Take profit at the opposite ±1.0σ band — small 1:1.5–1:1.8 reward, but a 72–78% win-rate profile.
The overnight engine trades small, frequent edges in the drift — never the violent NY open. Keep size small and stops tight.

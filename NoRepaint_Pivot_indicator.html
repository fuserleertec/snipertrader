The Daily Workflow

From chart to decision. In four steps.

You don't need to know how the script is built — you need to know how to trade it. Every session follows the same four-step read, so a setup becomes a decision in under a minute.

Step 01

Anchor to fair value

Choose your anchor mode — confirmed swing, session, day, or week. The VWAP line and its three deviation bands mark institutional fair value, so you can see at a glance whether price is at value or stretched away from it.

Step 02

Read the trend cloud

The triple-EMA cloud is your directional filter. Green means look for longs, red means look for shorts, grey means stand aside. No setup is ever taken against the cloud.

Step 03

Confirm the swing

A swing high or low only prints after two right-side bars close — and once printed, it stays. No repainting, no disappearing arrows, no quietly shifting stops.

Step 04

Trade the band extremes

A close back above VWAP signals impulse; a push through +3σ or −3σ signals a stretched move. Each event fires its own alert on bar close, so you never have to guess.

One setup = one decision. Fair value, trend, and structure all agree before you risk a dollar — and each fires its own alert so the read is handed to you, not left to interpretation.

Trend States

Three states. One filter.

The triple-EMA cloud resolves every bar into one of three states. The state tells you which side of the market is allowed — so the cloud is the first gate every signal must pass.

BULL▲

VWAP Anchor Modes

Four ways to anchor value.

Where the VWAP starts matters as much as where it sits. Pick the anchor that matches how you trade, and the bands recalculate from there.

Confirmed SwingDefault

Anchor to structure

Resets on every confirmed pivot

Every time a swing high or low confirms, VWAP restarts from that pivot. The bands track the most recent structural leg — ideal for reading each move in isolation.

SessionIntraday

Anchor to the session

Resets on each session's first bar

VWAP restarts at the open of each session, giving you a fair-value reference that follows the institutional trading windows throughout the day.

DayDaily

Anchor to the day

Resets on each new day

A clean daily reference. Price above day VWAP is intraday strength; price below is weakness — the classic mean-reversion yardstick.

WeekSwing

Anchor to the week

Resets on each new week

The longest-horizon anchor. Weekly VWAP smooths out daily noise and shows where larger players are accumulating or distributing over the full week.

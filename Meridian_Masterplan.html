Infrastructure & Reality Check

Do not skip this. Most prop firm failures are plumbing failures, not signal failures.

0.2 Pick your prop firm BEFORE you build the plan

The firm's rules become hard constraints in your position-sizing math — not afterthoughts.

Extract these numbers and write them on a card:

  • Daily loss limit ($ and %)
  • Trailing / static max drawdown
  • Profit target
  • Consistency rule (e.g. "no single day > 50% of total profit")
  • Minimum trading days
  • News-trading restrictions
  • Instrument restrictions (some firms limit contracts on NQ/ES)

0.3 The sizing formula (memorize this)

Added note — micro contracts & rounding
Full-size$ per pointMicro$ per point
NQ$20MNQ$2
ES$50MES$5
YM$5MYM$0.50
GC$100MGC$10

Micros are one-tenth the $/pt of the full-size contract, so the formula allows ten times the contract count.

Fractional results always round DOWN. The example works out to 600 ÷ 240 = 2.5, which becomes 2 contracts.

You deliberately use 40% of the daily limit, not 100%. This gives you room for a second and third attempt on a bad day without breaching.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–3

Meridian Engine v2 Mastery

◎Goal
Learn the context layer in isolation. No order flow yet. You must be able to read regime and liquidity without the OFI crutch.

1.1 Week 1 — Regime Engine

The engine classifies every bar from three inputs: ATR relative strength, manual ADX(14), EMA ribbon 9/21/50 alignment.

RegimeWhat the engine doesYour job
TRENDTrades only with ribbon + cloud; no-chase blocks stretched entriesJoin pullbacks, never chase
RANGEFades premium/discount extremes; gets a fade-into-extremes exception to no-chaseSell premium, buy discount
TRANSITIONDemands a denser lattice before anything firesStand down or halve size
▶Drill
Bar Replay, 200 bars, ES + NQ. Call the regime out loud on each bar and say why.
◆Gate 1a
20 consecutive correct regime reads with correct reasoning.

1.2 Week 2 — Liquidity Memory Map

Confirmed sweeps register a pool with price, direction, intensity, and a decaying magnet. Magnet decays bar by bar, strengthens when price revisits and reverses with volume. Cap is 20 pools; high-magnet pools add lattice weight and can become TP3.

▶Drill
Mark every sweep. Track which pools get revisited and which decay to nothing. Log: did the opposing pool actually become TP3?
◆Gate 1b
You can point to the 3 most magnetically significant pools on any chart and justify each.

1.3 Week 3 — 3-Gate Core + Event Lattice (structure only)

The three gates clear in order:

  • Gate 1 — Bias: regime-aware direction
  • Gate 2 — Armed: confirmed liquidity sweep OR volume-backed VWAP reclaim OR bullish/bearish CHoCH. Arms expire after a preset number of bars — stale setups auto-drop.
  • Gate 3 — Zone retest: price taps an untapped FVG/OB while the lattice reads at least your minimum grade

Lattice grading (structure events only, in this phase):

GradeEvent typesAction
WATCH2Research only — never live size
PRIME3Standard size (this is your live minimum)
ELITE4Full size
SINGULAR5+ and a high-magnet pool in rangeMaximum size and RR

Also internalize: mutual exclusion — if long and short fire the same bar, the engine keeps the higher-density side or stands aside on a tie. You will do the same.

◆Gate 1c
In Bar Replay, pre-write the trade (direction, entry, SL, TP1–3) before the BUY/SELL label prints. Hit 15/20 correct gate sequences.
✔Phase 1 complete when
You can narrate a chart cold — regime → pools → arm → zone → grade — without the OFI layer.
Phase 2 · Weeks 4–6

Meridian OFI Stacked Imbalance Mastery

◎Goal
Layer order flow on top of the context you already own. Critically — no new gates. OFI events raise density, which raises grade.

2.1 Week 4 — CVD (the primitive)

Volume split by close vs. prior close, accumulated. 3-bar slope sets rising/falling state. Session high/low tracked live. Reset: None / Session / Daily.

▶Drill
On 100 bars, mark accumulation vs. distribution phases. Note how often CVD slope flip precedes or lags the regime change.

2.2 Week 5 — Absorption (Type 8) & Imbalance (Type 9)

EventTriggerVisualMeaning
Absorption ↑/↓Strong opposing delta + rejection at FVG/OB, magnet, or VWAP bandSmall green/red circleDemand absorbing supply / supply absorbing demand
Imbalance ↑/↓Strong directional delta + CVD agreement + BOS/CHoCH/VWAP reclaim/zone tapSmall cyan/magenta diamondConviction continuation, not just a level reaction
▶Drill
Classify every Abs and Imb on 100 bars. Which ones had a level behind them? Those are the ones that matter.

2.3 Week 6 — Stacked Imbalance (Type 10) — the differentiator

This is the whole reason to buy the OFI edition.

How it works:

  • request.footprint(fpTicks, 70, fpImbPct) reads true bid/ask at every price row
  • A buy imbalance flags when a row's buy volume ≥ threshold% of the sell volume of the row below it → diagonal, 3:1 default
  • A sell imbalance mirrors it above
  • ≥3 consecutive same-side rows = a stacked zone → pushed as lattice type 10
  • Optionally registers as a high-intensity magnet (intensity = stack length × volume ratio)
  • Aligned stacks earn a +1 grade nudge

Why diagonal, not same-row: best bid and best ask sit one tick apart. Comparing same-row mixes both sides of the spread and is dominated by microstructure noise. Diagonal isolates genuine initiative.

Tuning table (from the docs):

Instrumentticks/rowTarget rows/bar
ES / NQ4–2015–40
Crypto / large-range bars50–20015–40

If stacks fire too often, raise Imbalance threshold % from 300 → 400–500%.

◆Gate 2a
20 consecutive correct ·FP (native) stack reads. You must distinguish them from ·px (proxy) fallback reads and know the difference in reliability.
◆Gate 2b
Explain in writing why a stack that aligns with the lattice is stronger than a stack in isolation.
✔Phase 2 complete when
You can identify a stacked imbalance, state whether it's ·FP or ·px, and say whether it lands on a level you already care about.
Phase 3 · Weeks 7–9

Integration & Playbook Construction

Now fuse the layers. You are building a written, repeatable playbook — not vibes.

3.1 Preset mapping

Start on Preset = Auto (derives Scalp / Intraday / Swing / Position from your chart timeframe). Once you have 30+ logged trades, you can justify a manual preset. Not before.

3.2 Write 3 core setups

Each setup must be fully specified:

Suggested three: (A) Trend pullback with Imb + stack, (B) Range fade at premium/discount with Abs, (C) Silver Bullet sweep reversal with stack.

3.3 Session amplification rules

Sessions are amplifiers (×1.25 density), never AND-gates. You never wait for a window — you score higher inside one.

Phase 4 · Weeks 10–14

Backtest → Forward Test → Evaluation

4.1 Backtest (Weeks 10–11)

Use the companion strategy script. Run across at least 3 instruments × 2 timeframes.

Sample size: 100 trades minimum per configuration. Less than that is noise.

MetricMinimum to proceed
Profit Factor≥ 1.3
Max Drawdown≤ 60% of the firm's trailing DD
Win rateWhatever it is — but RR must compensate
Losing streak (max)Must be survivable under your 40%-of-daily-limit sizing

If low-timeframe noise inflates grades: raise minimum grade to ELITE, or widen FVG filters.

◆Gate 4a
100-trade backtest, PF ≥ 1.3, max DD inside prop limits, max losing streak survivable.

4.2 Forward test on sim (Weeks 12–13)

20 trades, real-time, sim account, no exceptions. Backtests flatter you; live bar-close timing does not.

Track the Path Integrity stats specifically:

  • % of signals that hit PATH CONFIRM (TP1 touched first)
  • % that hit PATH BREAK (close beyond stop before TP1)
  • If BREAK rate > 45%, your entries are too late or your stops too tight.
◆Gate 4b
20 forward trades with forward-test PF within 25% of backtest PF. Divergence beyond that means curve-fitting.

4.3 Evaluation (Week 14)

Non-negotiable evaluation rules:

RuleSetting
Minimum gradePRIME (never WATCH live)
Max contractsFrom the 40% formula
Max trades/day3
Stop trading for the dayAfter 2 consecutive losses or −1.5%
Sessions traded08:00–12:00 ET only
InstrumentOne. Do not switch.

Consistency rule handling: If your firm requires "no day > 50% of total profit," your daily profit cap must be ≈ 50% of the target. Example: $6,000 target → stop the day at $3,000.

◆Gate 4c
Evaluation passed.
Phase 5 · Ongoing

Funded Account Management

Passing is the easy part. The failure mode is doubling size after funding.

5.1 The first 10 funded days

  • Trade the same contract count you used in the evaluation
  • No new setups
  • Same sessions, same minimum grade
  • Re-log everything

5.2 Scale-up ladder

MilestoneAction
10 funded days, no rule breach+25% size
30 funded days, no breach, PF ≥ 1.3+25% size
Any rule breachReset to evaluation size for 10 days
Payout takenReset to evaluation size for 5 days

Size increases only ever follow rule adherence — never follow a winning streak.

Failure Modes & Kill Switches

Chasing entries in TRENDNo-chase blocks stretched entries. If the label didn't print, there's no trade.

Trading ·px proxy stacks as if they're ·FPTag every read in the journal. Discount proxy reads by one grade mentally.

The One-Paragraph Version

Weeks 1–3: Meridian Engine v2 Mastery — learn regime, liquidity magnets, and the 3-gate sequence until you can narrate a chart without the OFI layer. Weeks 4–6: Meridian OFI Stacked Imbalance — add CVD, absorption, imbalance, and the native diagonal footprint stack (type 10), the strongest initiative signal in the system. Weeks 7–9: write three setups and a daily cadence. Weeks 10–14: 100-trade backtest (PF ≥ 1.3), 20-trade forward test, then evaluation at PRIME minimum grade, one instrument, 08:00–12:00 ET, 40%-of-daily-limit sizing. Then manage the funded account with an adherence-based scale ladder — never a streak-based one.

The engine gives you regime, grade, and a tracked path from entry to TP3. It does not give you discipline. That's the part you're actually building.

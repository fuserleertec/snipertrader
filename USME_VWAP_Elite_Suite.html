The Daily Workflow

From chart to decision. In four steps.

You don't need to know how the script is built — you need to know how to trade it. Every session follows the same four-step read, so a setup becomes a decision in under a minute.

Step 01

Anchor to fair value

Pick your session or day anchor. The VWAP line and its three deviation bands mark institutional fair value — so you can see at a glance whether price is at value or stretched away from it.

Step 02

Read the trend cloud

The EMA cloud is your directional filter. Green means look for longs, red means look for shorts. No setup is ever taken against the cloud.

Step 03

Wait for structure

BOS and CHOCH confirm the shift before you act — and every pivot prints on a closed bar and stays there. No repainting, no disappearing signals.

Step 04

Take the tier

The Signal-of-the-Day engine grades the setup WATCH, PRIME, or ELITE — with a matching alert and a matching size. More conviction, more size; less conviction, less.

One setup = one decision. Fair value, trend, structure, and tier all agree before you risk a dollar — and each fires its own alert so you never have to guess.

ABOUT THIS TERMINAL STATIC SNAPSHOT

Welcome to the SniperTrader market desk. Here's what it does, in plain terms: it watches the biggest stocks, the major cryptocurrencies, and the key futures contracts, pulls each one's recent price history, and reads that price action to judge which way each market is leaning and how strongly. It then ranks every market from highest to lowest conviction and, for each, maps out where a reasonable entry, a protective stop, and a profit target would sit. Click any row to open the full read — the reasoning, the exact levels, and a live chart.

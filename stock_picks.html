Conviction Engine & Stock Picks

Aggregate verified signals into a normalized Composite Conviction Score (0–100), map it to an execution tier, size the position with Fractional-Kelly + hard guardrails — then open any pick on a live TradingView chart. Live server-side recon; no orders are placed.

Conviction Engine & Stock Picks — a unified decision-support terminal that turns raw market signals into disciplined, risk-bounded trades.

  • The Conviction Scoring Engine aggregates verified signals across four sub-agents — Insider/Inside Buying (0.30), Technical Trend & Order Flow (0.30), Volume Surge (0.20), and Fundamental Catalyst (0.20) — into a single Composite Score (0–100). That score maps to a clear execution tier: Ultra-High (≥88) fires an immediate simulated order, High (78–87) scales in via staggered limits, Moderate (65–77) is watchlist-only paper, and anything below 65 is dropped. Position sizing uses a Fractional-Kelly model capped by hard guardrails — 30% sector limit, 70% total deployment / 30% cash floor, 5-position max, and a correlation >0.85 halving rule. Trade levels anchor to live ATR and swing structure.
  • Categorized Stock Picks surfaces the live watchlist as a filterable card grid (Ultra-High / High / Watchlist), each card one click from a TradingView light-box with its embedded advanced chart. Below that, the Recon Audit shows full funnel transparency — every scanned ticker that failed the conviction gate and why. Insider signals come from real SEC Form 4 filings (key-less); congressional STOCK Act data is included only when a Quiver key is configured.

Educational simulation only. Not financial advice. Everything runs against live public data — no orders are placed, no positions opened.

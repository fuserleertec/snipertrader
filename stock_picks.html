📚 Understanding the Simulation Engine

🔑 Kronos Patterns

BOS (Break of Structure), FVG (Fair Value Gap), and Breakout are ICT concepts detected from live price action. Higher confluence = higher structural conviction.

🐟 MiroFish Swarm

100 simulated agents model smart-money vs retail behavior. The swarm consensus is a Monte-Carlo simulation, not real order flow — used to stress-test narrative scenarios.

🎲 Probability Matrix

Three cones (Bull / Base / Bear) show what could happen with X% probability, derived from the conviction score + swarm bias. Not a price target.

⚖️ Fractional-Kelly

Position size = tier base allocation × Kelly fraction, capped by hard guardrails (30% sector, 70% total, 5 positions, correlation halving).

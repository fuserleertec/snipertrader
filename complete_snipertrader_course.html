1.1 Selecting Anchor Periods & Calculation Inputs

The foundation of the USME VWAP Suite is selecting the correct anchor period for your execution timeframe:

  • Session VWAP (Intraday): Resets at market open (e.g., 09:30 EST for equities/futures). Ideal for 1m–15m day trading.
  • Weekly / Monthly VWAP: Provides macro bias on 1H–4H charts. Identifies swing institution levels.
  • Event / Swing Anchor: Anchor directly to high-volume swing highs, swing lows, or major catalyst bars (FOMC, CPI).
Rule of Thumb: Never trade against the primary Session VWAP line without structural confirmation (e.g., liquidity sweep + market structure shift)[cite: 1].
Module 02 · Signal Interpretation

Reading VWAP Bands & Volume Signals

2.1 Reading Deviation Band Boundaries

Standard deviation bands show market sentiment and statistical extremes:

  • Fair Value Zone (VWAP to ±1σ): Balance zone. Price tends to chop or accumulate within this range.
  • Expansion Zone (±1σ to ±2σ): Moderate trend expansion. Retests of ±1σ in a trending market offer high-probability trend continuation setups.
  • Exhaustion Zone (±3σ Extreme): Overextended price. Statistically prone to mean-reversion rejections back toward VWAP.

Complimentary Trading Strategies

Strategy A ICT Liquidity Sweep + VWAP Discount Retest

Combines ICT market structure sweeps with USME VWAP fair value discount entries[cite: 1].

  1. Liquidity Sweep: Price sweeps Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) below a key swing low[cite: 1].
  2. Displacement & VWAP Cross: Strong impulse candle breaks back above the VWAP line[cite: 1].
  3. Retest Entry: Limit buy order placed at the −1σ VWAP band or VWAP line pullback (Discount zone)[cite: 1].
  4. Stop Loss: Placed below the liquidity sweep low[cite: 1].
  5. Take Profit Target: TP1 at +1σ Band; TP2 at +3σ Band[cite: 1].

Strategy B Statistical +3σ Mean Reversion Scalp

Capitalizes on statistical exhaustion when price reaches outer VWAP deviation bands during balanced market conditions.

  1. Band Extension: Price touches or pierces the +3σ upper VWAP band.
  2. Volume Exhaustion: Delta divergence appears (price makes high, delta fails to make new high).
  3. Trigger: Reversal candle closes back inside the +2σ band.
  4. Stop Loss: Placed 2–3 ticks above the extreme high wick[cite: 1].
  5. Take Profit Target: Mean reversion back to the main VWAP line.
